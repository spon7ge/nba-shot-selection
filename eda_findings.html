    What this does not show

    • Distance bands are analytical groups, separate from official NBA shot zones. Two-pointers split at 4, 10, and 16 feet. Every three-pointer stays in one group.
    • A shot on the corner side of the 22-foot line is a corner three even when it is closer than the 23.75-foot arc.
    • Coordinates treat xLegacy and yLegacy as tenths of a foot from the basket. Shots past half court stay in the team totals and off the drawing.
    • The notebook’s grid maps leave efficiency squares under 20 attempts blank. This page keeps those shots in the section total and shows the attempt count.
    • Heaves and free throws sit outside the attempt set. Possessions, turnovers, the shooter, the defender, and shot difficulty are outside this pass.
    • The scope is the 2025-26 regular season.
